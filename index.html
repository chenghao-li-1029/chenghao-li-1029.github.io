03 / Publications

Selected publications

Google Scholar ↗

My earlier research explored local features, neural scene representations, and real-time robotic perception. These publications appear under the name Shenghao Li. * Corresponding author.

  • Representing Boundary-ambiguous Scene Online with Scale-encoded Cascaded Grids and Radiance Field Deblurring
    Shenghao Li, Zeyang Xia, Qunfei Zhao*
    IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Video Technology (TCSVT), 2024
    paper project page

    An online scene representation method that handles unknown camera poses, boundary ambiguity, and observation noise such as camera motion blur, producing sharp and compact representations of scenes under a wide range of shooting conditions.

  • Sparse-to-Local-Dense Matching for Geometry-Guided Correspondence Estimation
    Shenghao Li, Qunfei Zhao*, Zeyang Xia
    IEEE Transactions on Image Processing (TIP), 2023
    paper code

    A fully differentiable correspondence estimation method guided by epipolar geometry. S2LD asymmetrically establishes correspondences with consistent sub-pixel coordinates while reducing the cost of matching; salient features are explicitly located, descriptions are conditioned on both views via attention, and correspondences are refined progressively across multiple levels to reduce re-projection error.

  • Quantized Self-supervised Local Feature for Real-time Robot Indirect VSLAM
    Shenghao Li, Shuang Liu*, Qunfei Zhao, Qiaoyang Xia
    IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics (TMECH), 2022
    paper

    A quantized self-supervised local feature for indirect VSLAM that stays robust to environmental interference in robot localization, while running in real time.

More publications 4 earlier papers
