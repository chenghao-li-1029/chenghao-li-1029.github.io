Chenghao (Cade) Li is Research Lead at Pika, where he works on video foundation models, post-training, and real-time audio-driven generation. He led PikaStream 1.0 and Pikaformance, led pretraining and SFT of Pika 2.2, and co-led training of Pika 2.5. Previously, he worked on Hailuo video and image foundation models at MiniMax. He received his Ph.D. in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in December 2023. His earlier publications appear under the name Shenghao Li.
